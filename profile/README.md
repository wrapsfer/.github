Wrapsfer is an order, stock and warehouse fulfilment platform for online merchants, run by Resetrix Pte. Ltd.

- **Marketplaces:** Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop and Shopify, with orders pulled in and stock pushed out automatically.
- **Warehouse fulfilment:** merchants store goods in the Resetrix warehouse, and the warehouse picks, packs and ships their orders.
- **Standalone:** merchants use Wrapsfer to manage their own orders and stock.
